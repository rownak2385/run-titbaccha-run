# Story Illustrations Specification

**Directory:** `/public/assets/story/`  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Recommended: 960×540 or 1280×720)  
**Formats Supported:** `.webp`, `.png`, `.jpg`  
**Fallback System:** If any file is missing, `IllustratedStoryPanel.tsx` automatically renders a high-quality, lightweight thematic SVG illustration with zero missing-image artifacts.

## Required Story Moments & File Slots:

1. `prologue_friendship.webp`: Ronny and Titi playing peacefully among sunflowers in the sunny garden.
2. `meteor_disaster.webp`: Violet celestial meteor streaking down and shattering the garden terraces.
3. `ch1_betrayal.webp`: The sly red fox offering golden bait to hungry Titi as Ronny walks away in sorrow.
4. `ch2_search.webp`: Titi following the golden heart seeds dropped from Ronny's traveling pack.
5. `ch3_memory_photo.webp`: The cherished Polaroid photo of Ronny holding baby Titi in the forest.
6. `ch4_dark_cave.webp`: Deep subterranean crystal cavern, torchlight glow, and train ticket fragments.
7. `ch5_moving_train.webp`: Highland Express speeding across mountain passes with Titi leaping across rooftops.
8. `ch6_seaside_explosion.webp`: Coastal terminus dock, engine boiler explosion, and destination map fragments.
9. `ch7_necklace_glow.webp`: Synchronized radiant emerald aura pulsing from matching carved leaf necklaces.
10. `ch7_reunion_hug.webp`: Ronny kneeling and cradling Titibaccha in a warm, tearful, emotional reunion embrace.
11. `sunset_ending.webp`: Ronny and Titi sitting together upon the emerald cliff overlooking the ocean sunset.
